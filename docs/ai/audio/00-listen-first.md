# PARTISAN AI — Spoken Briefing

**A narratable adaptation of the AI Compendium.** Six parts, roughly two hours end to end.
Written for listening: no tables, no code, no cross-references, numbers spoken as words, and a
recap at the end of every section. Feed to text-to-speech or read aloud.

| Part | File | Covers | Listen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `part-1-thesis.md` | The argument. Why smarter AI is the wrong target | ~16 min |
| 2 | `part-2-corpus.md` | The history. Pac-Man to Helldivers, and the unclaimed synthesis | ~26 min |
| 3 | `part-3-belief-individual.md` | The belief model and the individual soldier | ~22 min |
| 4 | `part-4-squad-faction-director.md` | Squads, the three faction brains, the director | ~21 min |
| 5 | `part-5-legibility-companions.md` | Barks, telegraphs, and your own squad | ~18 min |
| 6 | `part-6-engine-roadmap.md` | Unreal 5.8, the eight phases, and the honest part | ~23 min |

If you only have twenty minutes, listen to part one. If you have forty, one and six.

Content matches Compendium v1.0.0. Where the two differ, the written volumes are authoritative
— this version trades precision for narratability in places, and drops the tuning tables,
the code, the tactic sheet and the bibliography entirely. Those are in `../90-appendices.md`.

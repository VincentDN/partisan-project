# Appendix A — The tactic sheet

Eighteen tactics. Each carries preconditions, role slots, an abort condition, a signature bark
set and an authored ground shape. This is a content table, not an algorithm, and that is the
point (Vol IX §2.4).

| # | Tactic | Factions | Preconditions | Roles | Abort when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `FixAndFlank` | all | ≥4 effective, flank route exists, belief conf >0.6, morale >0.5 | BaseOfFire ×2, FlankElement ×2 | Flank element takes 50% losses, or belief conf <0.3 |
| 2 | `BoundForward` | REDFOR, BLUFOR | Advantage, suppression <0.3, ammunition healthy | Alternating pairs | Any pair pinned |
| 3 | `BoundBack` | all | Casualties taken or morale falling | Pairs, anchored | Reached cohesion anchor |
| 4 | `SuppressAndWait` | all | Low ammunition or no flank route | BaseOfFire all | Reinforcement arrives, or ammunition critical |
| 5 | `SearchPattern` | all | Belief conf <0.4 | Sweep line | New stimulus, or search exhausted |
| 6 | `HoldCutPoint` | REDFOR | On the road network, defensive posture | Static, mutually supporting | Position outflanked |
| 7 | `Rush` | all | Zealot leader, range <25m, morale >0.7 | All | Two down |
| 8 | `WithdrawToAnchor` | all | Leader dead or morale <0.35 | Disordered | Anchor reached or morale recovered |
| 9 | `Surrender` | REDFOR, GREF | Isolated, no ammunition, morale at floor, offer available | — | Offer withdrawn |
| 10 | `Ambush` | GREF | Terrain knowledge, target predicted on a route, concealment good | Trigger, Cutoff, Support | Triggered, or target route changed |
| 11 | `Fade` | GREF | Outmatched, uphill route available | All | Contact broken |
| 12 | `Cordon` | BLUFOR | Numbers ≥2:1, area bounded | Perimeter, Sweep | Perimeter breached |
| 13 | `CallForFire` | REDFOR, BLUFOR | Indirect asset available, belief age <45s | Spotter + all | Spotter killed, or fire delivered |
| 14 | `Overwatch` | GREF, BLUFOR | Elevated position, no immediate contact | Observer, Security | Contact, or relieved |
| 15 | `Harass` | GREF | Long range, ammunition healthy, escape route | Shooter ×1–2, Security | Return fire effective |
| 16 | `RecoverCasualty` | all | Friendly down, Loyalty check passed, no firing-line violation | Carrier ×2, Cover | Under effective fire |
| 17 | `Barricade` | REDFOR | In a building, outnumbered, no withdrawal route | All static | Position breached |
| 18 | `DemandSurrender` | BLUFOR, GREF | Enemy morale visibly low, range <40m | Speaker + all | Refused, or accepted |

Tactics 16 and 18 are the pillar-three tactics. Build them even if the set has to shrink
elsewhere.

# Appendix E — Glossary

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **Belief** | An agent's or squad's model of where a target is, with uncertainty, confidence, source and age. Never the truth |
| **Belief centroid** | The position a belief points at |
| **Bark** | An AI vocalisation that communicates a decision. The tactic's UI, not commentary |
| **Chokepoint API** | The single function through which all target-position information passes |
| **Cohesion anchor** | The position or agent a squad falls back toward; usually the leader |
| **Commit time** | Minimum duration a squad holds a tactic before re-scoring. Produces bait-ability |
| **Confidently wrong** | Acting decisively on a specific, plausible, incorrect belief. The most human-reading behaviour available |
| **Effective** | An agent that is alive, unbroken and not suppressed past a threshold |
| **Engine-agnostic library** | The portion of the AI written with no Unreal dependency |
| **Fabricated source** | A perceptible in-world justification the director attaches to an injected belief |
| **Legibility** | The property that a decision is perceivable by the player before its effect |
| **Mistake budget** | An explicit per-engagement allowance of authored errors |
| **Property 1–6** | The six human-reading properties. Vol 0 |
| **Spotlight** | The director state that suppresses allied lethality during a player's 1v1 |
| **Stale belief** | A belief past its useful age, still being acted upon |
| **Telegraph** | The observable signal preceding a decision's effect |
| **Two-brain rule** | Director has knowledge and no authority; agents have authority and no knowledge |
